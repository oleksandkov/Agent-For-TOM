# Final verification

1. Reopen the final DOCX and update fields, contents, references, and page numbers.
2. Export the PDF from that exact DOCX; do not reuse an older PDF.
3. Confirm DOCX/PDF page counts and inspect every rendered page, including the title page, contents, annotation, section starts, sources, and appendices.
4. Check for clipping, overflow, blank pages, orphaned headings, excessive gaps, broken tables, distorted figures, duplicate captions, and pages ending with a figure or caption.
5. Inspect every diagram at readable zoom: labels, arrows, line crossings, resolution, caption spacing, and first-reference proximity.
6. Confirm every contents entry and page number after the last layout change.
7. Confirm source numbering matches in-text citations and that electronic links remain active.
8. Compare package media before/after when only figures were authorized to change; unrelated DOCX parts must remain identical.
