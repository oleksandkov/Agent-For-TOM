# Technical diagram rules

- Redraw text-heavy block schemes and UML diagrams deterministically as SVG; export a 2x-or-higher PNG only when Word compatibility requires it.
- Use a white background, dark navy/slate outlines, restrained pale fills, one sans-serif font, consistent arrowheads, and generous whitespace.
- Keep labels exact, concise, and readable at the final Word size. Avoid gradients, decorative shadows, crossed connectors, clipped text, and ambiguous arrow directions.
- Use standard notation: rounded terminals, rectangles for actions, diamonds for decisions, dashed lifelines for sequences, compartments for UML classes, and labeled transitions for state diagrams.
- Keep captions and figure numbering in Word. Do not duplicate the title or `Рисунок ...` inside the artwork.
- Preserve aspect ratio, center the visual, and keep it inside margins. Retain the SVG source beside the exported PNG.
- Verify spelling, connector routing, and legibility after export and again in the rendered final PDF.
