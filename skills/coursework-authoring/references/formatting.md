# Formatting rules

Use the project's methodological files as the authority. The defaults below reflect the supplied guide and approved OOP sample.

## Page and body text

- A4 portrait; margins: left 2.5 cm, right 1 cm, top and bottom 2 cm.
- Times New Roman 14 pt; 1.5 line spacing; justified body text.
- First-line indent 1.25 cm; 0 pt before and after.

## Annotation and contents

- Center and bold `АНОТАЦІЯ`; add one separate empty paragraph before its body text.
- Center and bold `ЗМІСТ`; add one empty paragraph after it.
- Format contents like the approved sample: a borderless two-column layout, titles left and page numbers right, no dot leaders, no bold entries, 14 pt and 1.5 spacing.

## Headings

- Start each main section on a new page; use centered bold uppercase text without a final period.
- Use left-aligned regular subsection headings with a 1.25 cm indent and no final period.
- Place one separate empty paragraph between a section title and its first subsection, and one before and after each subsection heading. Do not imitate these gaps with paragraph spacing.

## Figures and tables

- Refer to each figure before it appears. Center it within the text area.
- Put its caption directly below, centered, Times New Roman 14 pt, 1.5 spacing: `Рисунок 3.1 – Назва` without a final period.
- After a caption, add one empty paragraph and substantive text. A page must not end with only a figure or caption.
- Put a table title above it, left-aligned with a 1.25 cm indent: `Таблиця 2.1 – Назва`.
- Keep table rows together. Repeat the header row only when a split is unavoidable. Keep the title with the table and add one empty paragraph plus analysis after it.

## Sources

- Start `СПИСОК ВИКОРИСТАНИХ ДЖЕРЕЛ` on a new page, centered, bold, uppercase, followed by one empty paragraph.
- Use one left-aligned paragraph per source, 1.25 cm first-line indent, 1.5 spacing, and 0 pt before/after.
- Keep numbering consistent with in-text references. Include an active URL and access date for online sources.
